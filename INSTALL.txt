Master Quartermaster - Bedrock Addon
====================================

INSTALLATION:
1. Download both folders: Master_Quartermaster_BP and Master_Quartermaster_RP
2. Move them to your Bedrock Add-ons folder:
   - Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\development_behavior_packs\ and development_resource_packs\
   - Console: Add through marketplace
3. Enable in world settings
4. Summon with: /summon mq:master_quartermaster

FEATURES:
- 22 Complete Trades
- All items fully enchanted (Level 30)
- Each trade = 27 stacks in a shulker box
- Works as Village Nitwit

TRADES:
Combat (7): Bows, Crossbows, Netherite Swords, Axes, Tridents, Maces, Shields
Tools (4): Pickaxes, Shovels, Hoes, Flint and Steel
Armor (5): Helmets, Chestplates, Leggings, Boots, Elytras
Consumables (6): Fireworks, Enchanted Golden Apples, Golden Apples, Golden Carrots, XP Bottles, Ender Pearls

VERSION: 1.0.0
MINECRAFT: 1.20.0+
